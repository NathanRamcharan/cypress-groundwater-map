# Cypress Groundwater & Subsidence Explorer

An interactive map and research project examining groundwater decline, land subsidence, and flooding in Cypress, Texas — built from public data (TWDB, USGS, HGSD, HCFCD, FEMA), field verification, and direct correspondence with agency staff.

**Live site:** https://nathanramcharan.github.io/cypress-groundwater-map/

## What's here
- **Map Explorer** — 20 groundwater wells, 12 subsidence stations, and 6 Cypress Creek gages, each with real agency data and source links
- **Evidence for Thesis** — the data laid out against the project's central question, including field photos from an in-person site visit
- **Research Paper** — a full write-up: introduction, literature review, methods, findings, discussion, conclusion, and works cited

## Research question
Is Cypress's public monitoring network adequate for the growth and environmental change the area has experienced since the mid-1980s?
